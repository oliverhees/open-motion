# Code of Conduct

We want everyone to feel welcome here, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be friendly and respectful
- Give and accept constructive criticism
- Take other viewpoints seriously
- Own your mistakes and learn from them
- Put the community before your ego

## What is not okay

- Insults, belittling, personal attacks
- Harassment, public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Anything inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report violations via [aiianer.de](https://aiianer.de) or confidentially to the maintainers. Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban people.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
